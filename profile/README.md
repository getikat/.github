**Ikat** is a personal-finance app for iPhone. It gathers your banks, cards, brokerage, retirement accounts and loans in one place, then tells you what changed, what's coming and what to do about it.

### Why "Ikat"?

[Ikat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ikat) is cloth woven from threads that were dyed *before* weaving. Each thread carries a small piece of the pattern, and the picture only appears once they are all in the loom together.

Your money is the same. A bank here, a card there, a 401(k) somewhere else: loose threads. Ikat weaves them into one picture.

### The short version

- **Your money stays on your phone.** Encrypted there, and backed up to your own iCloud, not to our servers.
- **It only looks.** Ikat reads your accounts and can't move a cent.
- **Nothing to type.** Link your banks once; categories, bills and subscriptions work themselves out.
- **No ads.** And we don't sell your data.
